
1. Make marinade
	a. Blend soy sauce, cilanro, garlic, white vinegar, oil, cumin, paprika, oregano and cayenne

2. Marinate chimkin for 30 mins - overnight

3. Cut baby potatoes

4. Season potatoes on pan

5. Bake potatoes 390 for 20 mins in air fryer

6. Blend green sauce stuff to make green sauce

7. Preheat oven 400 

8. Add chimkin to baking pan and add leftover marinade

9. Bake for 20-22 mins or until 165 degrees

10. Saute asparagus
