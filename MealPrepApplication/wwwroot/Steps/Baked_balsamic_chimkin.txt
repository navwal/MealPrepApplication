
1. Boil balsamic vinegar (med-low heat 4-5 mins)

2. pour in glass jar and cool in fridge

3. Add other salsa stuff to bowl

4. Drizzle balsamic vinegar over bowl

5. Mix vinegar, thyme and mustard in bowl

6. Marinade chimkin and cover for at least 30 mins

7. Preheat oven to 400 degrees

8. Place chimkin on baking dish and pour marinade over

9. Bake for 20-22 mins
