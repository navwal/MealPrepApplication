
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees

2. Mix meatball stuff

3. Form meatballs into 1 inch balls

4. Bake for 15-20 mins

6. Heat oil in pan and add onions and garlic (about 2-3 mins)

7. Add chimkin broth, heavy cream, tomato paste, Italian seasoning and red pepper flakes

8. Whisk till smooth

9. Stir in sun-dried tomatoes and parmesan cheese until cheese has melted

10. Let sauce simmer for 3-4 mins
