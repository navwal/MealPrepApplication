
1. Prep chimken: 
	A. Cut chimkin in bite sized pieces.
	B. Place in large bowl.
	C. Add chimkin stuff and coat.

2. Prep sauce:
	A. Whisk sauce stuff in small bowl.

3. Cook chimkin
	A. Oil, medium-high heat.
	B. 2-3 mins on each side
	C. Move chimkin out of pan

4. Make sauce
	A. Lower to medium heat
	B. Whisk sauce in pan
	C. Simmer 4-5 mins

5. Toss
	A. Add chimkin and cilantro to pan
	B. Toss until chimkin is fully coated
