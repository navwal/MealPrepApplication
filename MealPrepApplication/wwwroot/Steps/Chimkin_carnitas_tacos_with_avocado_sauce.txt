
1. Make sauce
	a. Blend sauce stuff until it's smoov

2. Make chimkin carnitas
	a. Place chimkin in large bowl
	b. mix chimkin stuff in bowl
	c. Marinate for 30 mins - 8 hrs
	d. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
	e. Bake in baking dish for 15-20 mins
	f. Discard marinade

3. Make tacos
	a. Turn oven to 425 degrees
	b. Line with large baking sheet and parchment paper
	c. Lay tortillas on pan and spray with cooking spray
	d. Flip over and bake for 2-3 mins
	e. Take pan out and add cheese, chimkin, red onion and a lil more cheese
	f. Fold and make taco stick that way
	g. Place pan in for 15-17 mins
