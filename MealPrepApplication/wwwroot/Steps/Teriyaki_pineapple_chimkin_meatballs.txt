
1. Make sauce
	a. Add soy sauce, garlic, honey, rice vinegar to sauce pot
	b. Boil on med heat and stir to mix in honey
	c. Once boiling, mix cornstarch and water to sauce pot and stir

2. Make meatballs
	a. Preheat 350 degrees
	b. Make 1 inch meatballs put of 1st part
	c. Bake meatballs for 25-30 mins

3. Saute
	a. Saute peppers and serve
