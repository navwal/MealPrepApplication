
1. Marinate Chimkin in stuff above

2. Preheat oven to 415 degrees

3. Boil potato chunks till tender

4. Make dressing:
	a. mix lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil, oregano, chili flakes, salt and honey.

5. Oven roast potatoes
	a. Add half dressing to potatoes.
	b. Roast on baking sheet for 20 mins
	c. Stir and add rest of dressing
	d. Roast for another 20 mins

6. Sizzle chimkin on medium heat (6-7 mins each side)
