
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees

2. Prepare Zucchini
	a. Cut off the top stem of each zucchini and slice each zucchini in half length-wise
	b. Use a spoon to scoop out the insides leaving about a quarter inch of zucchini on all sides

3. Make the filling
	a. Heat a slash of oil in large saute pan med-high heat
	b. Once hot add garlic and onion until tender (2-3 mins)
	c. turn burner to med
	d. Add chimkin, black beans, cilantro, chili powder, cumin and oregano
	e. Stir Enchilada sauce

4. Assemble
	a. Spread some extra enchilada sauce over the bottom of your prepared dish
	b. Place the zucchini cut side up in the dish
	c. Divide the filling evenly among the zucchini boats and cover the dish with aluminum foil

5. Bake
	a. Bake for 25-30 mins (Zucchini should be tender and the filling should be hot all the way through)
	b. Uncover and sprinkle cheddar cheese over each zucchini boat
	c. Place the dish back in the oven and bake for an additional 4-5 mins or until cheese has melted
