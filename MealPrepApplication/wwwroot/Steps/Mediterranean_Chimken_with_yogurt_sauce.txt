
1. Marinate the chimken:
	a. Mix garlic, Greek yogurt, lemon juice, tomato paste, smoked paprika, olive oil, cumin, dried oregano, salt and black pepper together in a large bowl
	b. Add the chimkin pieces and marinate for 1-4 hours (or up to overnight).

2. Bake:
	a. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
	b. Add the bell peppers and onions to a large rimmed sheet pan
	c. Toss in 1 tbsp of olive oil and generously season with salt
	d. Spread the veggies out on the pan once done
	e. Place the marinated chimkin pieces on top of/around the veggies and bake for 15-20 mins
	(Optional). turn the oven to broil for the last 3 mins to get a little char

3. Make the sauce
	a. While the sheet pan bakes, mix the Greek yogurt, lemon juice, garlic and chopped dill together
	b. Season with salt to taste
	c. Store in the refrigerator

4. Serve
	a. Spoon the chimken/veggie mixture over steamed white rice with a big scoop of sauce on top
	b. Or add big dollops of sauce to the sheet pan and serve with lemon wedges
