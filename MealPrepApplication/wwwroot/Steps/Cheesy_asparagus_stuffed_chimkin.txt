
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees

2. Blend pesto ingredients until smooth

3. Cut chimkin breasts 3/4 way through

4. Season generously with paprika, salt and pepper

5. Heat skillet on med-high heat

6. Once hot, sear both sides (2-3 mins)

7. put chimkin in baking pan

8. Fill chimkin with asparagus, cheese and sun dried tomato

9. Bake for 20-22 mins or until cheese is melted

10. Put pesto on chimkin
